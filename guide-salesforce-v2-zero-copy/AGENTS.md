## Architecture

This documentation-only guide explains both directions of Salesforce and
Snowflake zero copy. It distinguishes V2 Data Share access from Salesforce
query/file federation, legacy BYOL, Openflow replication, and Salesforce MCP.

## Conventions

- Use only public, stable documentation for externally shareable claims.
- Treat bidirectional zero copy as two directional configurations.
- Keep Data 360, Data Cloud, DLO, DMO, and CIO terminology explicit.
- Exclude internal-only migration flags and roadmap claims.
- Never add customer names, account identifiers, internal roadmap dates, or private-preview evidence.
