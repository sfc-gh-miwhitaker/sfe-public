# Restaurant Recovery Explorer, in Plain Language

Pair-programmed by SE Community + Cortex Code

> Simplified from: [Restaurant Recovery Explorer](README.md)

## One-Sentence Version

This fictional restaurant demo shows where visits fell, compares similar restaurants, and turns the evidence into a proposed next step.

## The Story

Think of a regional manager reviewing a stack of restaurant scorecards. One overall number hides busy locations, quiet breakfasts, new openings, and closures.

The explorer organizes those scorecards on a clickable map. Select a restaurant to see which occasions changed and how similar restaurants performed.

The action brief is a checklist backed by those numbers. It suggests what to investigate or test, not a proven explanation.

## The Cast

- **Guest occasions:** Visits, not unique people.
- **Peers:** Similar restaurants chosen using earlier history.
- **Evidence:** A number with its calculation, period, and source.
- **Action brief:** A template that separates observations, possible explanations, and missing information.

## What Changed

- Instead of one market total, inspect the restaurants and occasions behind it.
- Instead of guessing a comparison group, inspect the matching rules.
- Instead of an unsupported recommendation, follow links to its evidence.

## What to Watch Out For

Every restaurant and result is fictional. Similar trends do not prove cause. Missing records are not zero visits.

The app cannot identify returning guests, measure experiment success, or calculate profit. It runs locally without a database or AI connection.

## The One Thing to Remember

The demo helps choose what to investigate, not claim the answer is already known.
